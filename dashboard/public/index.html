Network access
Egress & local services

            
            

            
One hostname per line, without ports. Domain rules cover all HTTP(S) ports. Restricted HTTP(S) traffic uses nono’s proxy; clients must honor HTTP_PROXY / HTTPS_PROXY. Deny rules take priority.

            
Direct ports bypass domain filtering and permit connections to any IP on that port. Listen ports permit binding, including non-loopback addresses.
